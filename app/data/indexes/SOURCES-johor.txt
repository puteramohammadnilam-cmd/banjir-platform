Flash flood potential index (FFPI) of johor in this folder
============================================================

app/indexes.py reads these files. To make them again, from 11_Platform_Draft:

  .venv/bin/python tools/fetch_indexes.py worldcover --area johor   # and ndvi, soilgrids, worldpop
  .venv/bin/python tools/fetch_state_osm.py --area johor            # rivers, drains, roads
  .venv/bin/python tools/build_ffpi.py --area johor
  .venv/bin/python tools/build_section_scores.py --area johor

The fetch steps are needed once; the build reads only the saved files listed below and gives the same bytes
each time (inputs fetched 2026-11-28). The method is in tools/build_ffpi.py's docstring and in johor_ffpi.json.
