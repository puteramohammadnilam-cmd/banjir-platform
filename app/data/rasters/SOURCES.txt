JRC flood depth rasters in this folder
======================================

app/raster.py reads these files (from this folder, or from BANJIR_RASTER_DIR when that is set) to look up the
modelled flood depth at a point. They are about 70 MB, so .gitignore keeps the .tif files out of git and only this
list is committed. deploy.sh stops if one is missing, and tests/test_rasters_shipped.py checks each file below
against its size and SHA-256 (it skips a file that is absent). To put them back, copy them in:

  cp ../05_Raw_Data_Sources/jrc_glofas_v2_1/ID2*_RP10_depth.tif ../05_Raw_Data_Sources/jrc_glofas_v2_1/ID2*_RP20_depth.tif \
     ../05_Raw_Data_Sources/jrc_glofas_v2_1/ID2*_RP50_depth.tif app/data/rasters/

or download each one from the JRC portal, at <Download>/RP<10, 20 or 50>/<file name>:
  https://jeodpp.jrc.ec.europa.eu/ftp/jrc-opendata/CEMS-GLOFAS/flood_hazard/

The app shows three flood sizes, 1 in 10, 20 and 50 years. JRC v2.1 has larger ones too (1 in 75 years and up);
they are not shipped, and the page says floods bigger than 1 in 50 years are not shown.

Source:    European Commission JRC, global river flood hazard maps v2.1 (Baugh and others, 2024)
Link:      https://developers.google.com/earth-engine/datasets/catalog/JRC_CEMS_GLOFAS_FloodHazard_v2_1
Licence:   CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
Download:  https://jeodpp.jrc.ec.europa.eu/ftp/jrc-opendata/CEMS-GLOFAS/flood_hazard/
Coverage:  three grids of 10 x 10 degrees, all 0.008-10.007 N:
           N10_E100  99.976-109.975 E   Peninsular Malaysia except Langkawi, and the far west of Sarawak
           N10_E110  109.975-119.975 E  the rest of Sarawak, and Sabah
           N10_E90   89.977-99.976 E    Langkawi
           (rectangles, so they also take in Singapore, Brunei, and parts of Thailand, Sumatra and Kalimantan)
Date:      2026-10-04
Copy:      byte for byte from 05_Raw_Data_Sources/jrc_glofas_v2_1. The RP10 files: N10_E100 fetched 2026-09-30,
           N10_E110 and N10_E90 downloaded from the link above on 2026-10-03. The RP20 and RP50 files of all three
           grids downloaded from the link above on 2026-10-04 (headers, grid and tiles checked against the RP10 files:
           the same grid, smallest depth 0.10 m, and the wet squares nest from 1 in 10 to 1 in 20 to 1 in 50)
