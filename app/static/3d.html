How to use the 3D view
  • Move: drag the map (two fingers on a phone; one finger scrolls the page).
  • Turn and tilt: hold the right mouse button, or Ctrl, and drag: sideways turns, up and down tilts. On a phone, twist two fingers to turn and drag two fingers up or down to tilt.
  • Zoom: scroll or pinch, or use + and − at the top left. The arrow under them points north again.
  • Flood size: pick once in 10, 20 or 50 years; the water rises to that flood's depth.
  • Satellite photo: switch the photo under the buildings on or off.
  • Back: "← MyFlood" returns to the shop's card.
